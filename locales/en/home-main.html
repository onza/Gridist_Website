01 Window Memory

Monitor gone.
Layout stays.

No profile to save. Gridist remembers every window per monitor and restores it when you switch.

Desktop Monitor

Monitor off → layout back

  • Arrange & forget

    Arrange your windows once the way you want them — on your MacBook, at your desk, anywhere. Gridist remembers every position automatically per monitor.

  • Detect monitor changes

    Cable out or in — the display fingerprint changes, Gridist notices and loads the matching Window Memory.

  • Restore automatically

    Every open window jumps to its last remembered position on the active monitor. Percentage coordinates fit any resolution.

02 Window Snap

Drag. Release. Snap.

Grab the title bar and drag — a mini-monitor preview with your layout zones appears. Hover a cell, the ghost outline shows the result. Release — done.

  • Built-in sets: halves, quarters, thirds, two-thirds, full screen
  • Custom Layout Sets with overlapping zones
  • Option key toggles zones on and off
  • Editable shortcuts for presets and custom zones
